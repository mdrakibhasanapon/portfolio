01_about

      
A builder with a hacker's curiosity.

      
I got into tech the way a lot of people do: wondering what actually happens behind the screen. That question turned into a habit of taking systems apart, and eventually into cybersecurity and Capture The Flag competitions.

      
Alongside security, I'm a full-stack app developer. I enjoy the whole journey, from sketching an idea and designing the database to building the interface and getting it live. Knowing how attackers think makes me a more careful developer, and building real products keeps my security work grounded.

      
Right now I'm studying Computer Science & Engineering at Daffodil International University and competing as an elite member of Team NullX. I'm always up for a hard problem, a good team and a chance to learn something new.
